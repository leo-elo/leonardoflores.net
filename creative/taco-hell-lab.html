This work offers the opportunity for people to combine a limited number of ingredients to create new Taco Hell menu items.

Like Taco Hell Menu, it critiques Taco Bell's combinatorial approach to menu creation and its appropriation of Mexican foods, both emblematic of the USAmerican fast food industry.
