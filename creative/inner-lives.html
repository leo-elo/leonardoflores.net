"Inner Lives" generates a 4×3 colorful grid with 12 randomly chosen emoji, each of which is paired with a randomly selected word representing a mood or emotion. This piece invites reflection on the emotional valence of the people represented by emoji and on the inner lives of those we encounter every day.

Programmed in conversation with ChatGPT on June 11–14, 2023.
