Anti-Woke Lullaby is a generative digital poem that repurposes the structure of the traditional lullaby "Hush, Little Baby" to critique political complacency. I use cyborg writing practices to merge traditional writing with digital technologies, incorporating variables and generative code. The poem presents a series of contingent promises that mirror conservative attempts to maintain ignorance.

Written by Leonardo Flores using ChatGPT versions 03-mini-high and 5 to generate code. Completed on March 22, 2025.

Published in
