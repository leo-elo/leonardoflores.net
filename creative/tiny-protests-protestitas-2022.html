Tiny Protests / Protestitas is a reflection and comment on the difficult times we are living in. The work exists in four manifestations: @TinyProtests is a bot that generates US-themed marches and protests in Twitter, @Protestitas is a fork from the original that generates PR themed protests, Tiny Protests / Protestitas brings them together into a single, endless march, and Tiny Protests generates a 1,334 page march calling for Trump's impeachment, in book format for NanoNaNoGenMo. The slogans and march configurations for this bot are inspired by and drawn from real protests. With the return to political sanity in the US and to a lesser degree in PR, these bots will soon pause in their operations, until they are called to return to the virtual streets to fight once again for all who are oppressed.
