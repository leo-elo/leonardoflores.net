Ingobernable / Ungovernable is a bilingual interactive digital poem about collective resistance and political agency. A framed "poder" (power) div — bordered like an official document — presents a quote about what happens when those in power ignore the people. In response, diverse human emojis march up from the bottom of the screen, each carrying a word or phrase from a vocabulary of resistance: organización, desobediencia, obediencia maliciosa, unidad, solidaridad — and their English equivalents. The words detach from the marchers and settle into the canvas as colorful word art, accumulating into a visual manifesto. The work concludes with a call to action: ¡A las urnas! / To the polls!

A language toggle (🇵🇷 / 🇺🇸) switches the entire work between Spanish and English in real time, including all on-screen words, the power quote, and the finale — making it a genuinely bilingual piece rather than two parallel versions. Words can be dragged and repositioned by the reader. The work restarts automatically, generating a new configuration each time.

This work is dedicated to those who fight for our rights and a better future.

Written by Leonardo Flores and vibe coded with Claude Code. May 2026.

Thanks to Jorge Eliécer Gaitán, Sergio Ortega Alvarado, and Quilapayún for "El pueblo unido jamás será vencido." ("The people, united, shall never be defeated.")
