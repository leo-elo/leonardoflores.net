This work juxtaposes random mood emoji with two other generated mood affecting elements: color and sound. The result invites users to tap/click, drag, play, listen, gaze, and interpret the combination of these three elements. What moods can be evoked by these three parallel (yet intersecting) processes? Have fun creating mood music!

Created with help from Chat GPT 4 from September 10–15, 2023.
