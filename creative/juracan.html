"Juracán" generates situations before, during, and after a hurricane strike in the Caribbean. Named after the Taíno name for the weather event (and god), this piece is inspired by the Twitter bot @Huracan_PR, which was launched on the first anniversary of the passage of Hurricane María through Puerto Rico (September 20, 2019). This work is dedicated to all who have experienced, survived (or didn't), and continue to endure the aftermath of this and other similar weather events.

Credits: This work adapts the code of my earlier work "Tiny Protests/Protestitas" which in turn is based on Milton Läufer's "God" published in Taper #1, or as I like to call it, "the God code." I am very grateful for Milton Läufer and Angela Chang's help in developing this piece.
