Week 2: Teaching Literature Rhetorically

January 19, 2022

In class

  • Discussion of Teaching Literature Rhetorically, pgs. 1-121.
  • Group presentations (30 mins each):
    • Chapter 1: Integrating Skills and Knowledge
    • Chapter 2: Reading Closely and Critically
    • Chapter 3: Assessing the Rhetorical Situation
    • Chapter 4: Analyzing Genres
  • Lesson Plan and Teaching Demo

Assignments

  • Read these chapters:
    • Fletcher, Teaching Literature Rhetorically, Chapter 5: Negotiating Voices and Meaning
    • Showalter, Teaching Literature,
      • Chapter 2: Theories of Teaching Literature
      • Chapter 6: Teaching Fiction (in shared folder)
  • Read the following short stories (in shared folder):
    • "Wakefield" by Nathaniel Hawthorne - Caroline Turner
    • "Everyday Use" by Alice Walker
    • "Happy Endings" by Margaret Atwood
    • "The Black Cat" by Edgar Allan Poe
    • "Girl" by Jamaica Kincaid
  • Individually, write a 1-2 page discussion on what literary and rhetorical concepts you would focus on when teaching the short story assigned to your group, referencing Fletcher and Showalter. Post in Google Classroom before class next week.
    • Group 1: "Everyday Use" by Alice Walker
    • Group 2: "Happy Endings" by Margaret Atwood
    • Group 3: "The Black Cat" by Edgar Allan Poe
    • Group 4: "Girl" by Jamaica Kincaid
