In class:

  • Teaching Demo:

      • Script
      • Playlist for musical

  • Teaching critical memes & #macbethmemes
  • Teaching Shakespeare and Macbeth

      • Royal Shakespeare Company's 2003 production
      • Scotland, PA (2003)
      • Throne of Blood (1957)
      • The Tragedy of Macbeth (2021) (Apple TV)
  • Teaching full length plays

Assignment

  • Readings for Camaryn Crump's poetic form teaching demo:
    • “The Weary Blues” by Langston Hughes
    • (Read and Listen– I suggest at the same time) )
      • “Dope” by Amiri Baraka
      • Amiri Baraka - Dope
    • “We Real Cool” By Gwendolyn Brooks
  • Additional Readings:
    • Showalter, Teaching Literature, Chapter 4: Teaching Poetry (in shared folder)
    • Greene, Roland, et al., eds. The Princeton encyclopedia of poetry and poetics. Read entries on: "Poetry," "Form," "Prosody," "Scansion," "Meter," "Rhyme," "Alliteration," and "Free Verse"
    • Read the following poems:
      • "Introduction to Poetry" by Billy Collins
      • "Jabberwocky" by Lewis Carroll
      • "Tender Buttons: Objects" by Gertrude Stein
      • "[Because I could not stop for Death]" by Emily Dickinson
      • "The Dance" by William Carlos Williams
      • "[In Just-]" by E. E. Cummings
    • Write a brief (1-2 page) analysis of how the poet uses elements of sound, poetic form, visual structure, and/or word choices tactically to support their goals with the poem. You will choose a poem in class today. Reference relevant entries in the Princeton Encyclopedia. Post in our Google Classroom before next class.
