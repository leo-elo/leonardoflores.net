Week 5: Teaching Short Plays

February 9, 2022

In class

  • Teaching Demo with Brandon Ward
    • "Trifles" by Susan Glaspell
  • Discussion of "Sure Thing" by David Ives
    • Theatrical production (2017)
    • Short Film (2019)
  • Discussion of pedagogical readings:
    • Showalter Teaching Literature "Chapter 5: Drama"
    • Kay, Matthew R. Not light, but fire: How to lead meaningful race conversations in the classroom. (link to ebook) - Chapters 1 & 2
  • Discussion of "Dutchman" by Amiri Baraka
    • Short film (1966)
    • Theatrical production: part 1 & part 2 (2016)
    • Baraka commentary (2011)

Assignment

  • Reading/Viewing:
    • ”The Unfortunates” by Jon Beavers, Casey Hurt, Ian Merrigan, and Ramiz Monsef
      • Script
      • Playlist for musical
      • The making of the play
    • Read Macbeth
    • Watch the adaptation assigned to your group:
      • Royal Shakespeare Company's 2003 production
      • Scotland, PA (2003)
      • Throne of Blood (1957)
      • The Tragedy of Macbeth (2021) (Apple TV)
    • Macbeth Memes
      • Read "Teaching Critical Memes"
      • Create 2-3 memes using still or animated GIF images from your Macbeth adaptation/production. The memes need to quote, paraphrase, or riff on actual text from the play and offer commentary and/or insight on the play.
      • Suggested tools:
        • imgflip meme generator
        • GIPHY
        • PowerPoint
      • Embed your memes in a 1-2 page document in which you briefly discuss the memes. This is due before our next class.
      • Optional: share your memes in social media (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook) using the #macbethmemes hashtag.
