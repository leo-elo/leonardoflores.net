Weeks 8-9: Dancing Language

October 9, 2023

Monday, October 9

  • In class:
    • Discussion of Creative Assignment #2.
    • Lecture on the origins of video poetry:
      • Title Sequences:
        • Title Sequence for North by Northwest (1959) by Arthur Preminger
        • Pinky and the Brain Title Sequence (1995) by Bryan Evans, Tom Ruegger, et. al.
        • Cowboy Bebop (1998) by Cain Kuga
      • Video poetry
        • "Roda Lume" (1968) by E.M. de Melo e Castro
        • "Sign Language" (1985) by Tom Konyves
        • “The Dreamlife of Letters” (2000) and “Star Wars: One Letter at a Time” (2005) by Brian Kim Stefans
  • Assignment:
    • Read:
      • Explore Young-Hae Chang Heavy Industries
      • Write a post in which you discuss one of their video poems. Link to the video poem you discuss.

Wednesday, October 11

In class:

  • Discuss Young-Hae Chang Heavy Industries
  • Discuss video poetry:
    • “Bring Back” (2017) by Rosamond King
    • “The Offline Website Project” (2019-2020) by Alex Saum-Pascual
    • "Big Data Poetry" (2020) by Diego Bonilla and Rodolfo Mata
  • Discuss kinetic typography:
    • "Fuck You" by Cee Lo Green
    • "Right Action" by Franz Ferdinand (video by Jonas Odell)
    • EDMLyrics

Assignment:

  • Explore/Read:
    • #kinetictypography on Instagram
    • #kinetictypography on YouTube, focusing less on tutorials and more on actual kinetic typography and lyric videos.
  • Write a post in which you discuss one kinetic typography work in Instagram and one from YouTube. Provide links to the works.

Friday, October 13

Zoom Class (link in syllabus and ASULearn)

  • Be prepared to share and discuss your kinetic typography links and videos.
  • Assignment #2 due.
  • Midterm Self Assessment due (see ASULearn)

Assignment:

  • Enjoy Fall Break!

Monday, October 16

Fall Break: no class.

Wednesday, October 18

Zoom class

  • Lecture on kinetic e-poetry:
    • “Velocity” (2000) by Tina Escaja
    • “Mastering the Art of French Cooking and Systems Theory” (2015) by Tan Lin
    • "Thoughts Go" (2000) by David Knoebel
    • “Loss, Undersea” by D. Fox Harrell
    • “Acts in Translation” by Dalena Tran

Assignment:

  • Watch/Read:
    • Rettberg, Electronic Literature, Chapter 5: Kinetic and Interactive Poetry
    • “A Modern Ghost” by Artemio Morales
    • “The Vine and the Fish” by Leise Hook
    • “Upgrade Soul” by Ezra Claytan Daniels, Erik Loyer

Friday, October 20

Asynchronous Class

  • In our forum, write a post on two of the assigned works:
    • “A Modern Ghost” by Artemio Morales
    • “The Vine and the Fish” by Leise Hook
    • “Upgrade Soul” by Ezra Claytan Daniels, Erik Loyer
  • Post by Saturday, respond to a classmate's by Monday
