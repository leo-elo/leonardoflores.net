Weeks 9 & 10: Coping with Artificial Intelligence

March 16, 2026

Week 9: March 9-13

Spring Break -  no class

Monday, March 16

In Class

  • Discussion: AI and your career paths
  • AI Writer Jobs at Indeed.com
  • Student Guide to AI Literacy

Assignment

  • Write a forum post in which you discuss at least one way in which AI can enhance your skills and/or contribute to your career goals. Discuss at least one way in which it can interfere or harm you on your journey.

Wednesday, March 18

In Class

  • Discussion: Leveraging and coping with AI
  • Discussion: Capstone Paper

Assignment

  • Read: "The Five Stages of AI Grief"
  • Write a forum post in which you discuss how you feel about AI technologies and how Bratton's essay may help you identify and process some of your feelings (and others' responses) about AI. Post by noon on Friday.

Friday, March 20

Asynchronous Class Activity

  • Read at least 3-4 posts by your classmates and respond to at least 2 of them by Monday.

Assignment

  • Read: Capstone Paper
  • Write a proposal for your capstone paper. Post in forum before class on Monday.
