Weeks 7 & 8: Applying to Opportunities

February 25, 2026

Monday, February 23

In class - Class canceled due to weather

Assignment

Wednesday, February 25

  • Discussion of Nonprofit opportunities:
    • Indeed.com nonprofit jobs
    • Globaljobs.com
    • Idealist.org
    • The 15 Biggest NGOs in the World
    • Save the Children Careers
    • OXFAM Careers
  • Careers in Publishing:
    • Careers in Publishing
    • 13 Jobs to Work in Publishing
    • Bookjobs.com
    • Penguin Random House Careers
  • Application and Resume Assignment - Due March 6

Assignment

  • Write a post in which you discuss which job, program, fellowship, or opportunity you'd be interested in applying to, what it requires of you to successfully apply for it, and what career path you envision with it. Provide a link to the job opportunity. This will be what you will eventually turn in for your first graded assignment.
  • Post in our forum by noon on Friday.

Friday, February 27

Asynchronous Class Activity and Assignment
  • Drafting session: write a draft of your application essay and resume.
  • Post in our Forum before class on Monday, March 2.

Monday, March 2

In class

  • Discussion of drafts
  • Application and Resume Assignment - Due Friday, March 6
  • Midterm Self Assessment due Friday, March 6.

Assignment

  • Revise your draft taking into account feedback from your peers.

Wednesday, March 4

  • Final questions on drafts.
  • Discussion of emergent Career opportunities:
    • Google Search on Entrepreneurship for English Majors.
    • How to Become an Influencer in 10 Steps: Your 2026 Guide
    • 9 Artificial Intelligence (AI) Jobs to Consider in 2026
    • Discussion of emergent jobs and career opportunities

Assignment

  • Complete the Midterm Self Assessment by Friday, March 6.
  • Application and Resume Assignment - Due Friday, March 6.

Friday, March 6

Asynchronous Class Activity:

None: focus on turning in your final assignment.

Assignment

  • Write a reflection of how you think AI might affect your career path and what you can do about it.
  • These posts are due before class on Monday, after Spring Break.
Enjoy Spring Break!
