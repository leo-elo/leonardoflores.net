Capstone Paper

November 10, 2025

For this project you will either adapt a paper you wrote for a previous 4000-level literature course you took or an assignment for a 4000-level literature course you are currently taking and expand it to the length, critical engagement, and quality expected of a peer-reviewed publication. I will work with you on the writing process and in attuning the paper to the requirements and audience for the publication.
  • The capstone paper should be about 3000-4000 words long in MLA format (about 12-15 pages, longer is fine). You must incorporate at least 3 peer-reviewed secondary sources in your essay, which must incorporate a clearly articulated critical framework.
  • You must incorporate at least 3 peer-reviewed secondary sources in your essay, which must have a clearly articulated theoretical framework.
  • The Capstone Paper has 3 components:
    • An Annotated Bibliography
    • A Presentation on Capstone Day
    • The Capstone Paper
  • The learning objectives for this assignment are the following:
    • To develop a research paper beyond the audience/focus of a literature seminar, to meet the standards of a peer-reviewed publication in the field.
    • To develop your research skills so you can refine a search and identify useful scholarly resources to support your paper.
    • To assess and compile resources in support of your research project.
    • To clearly articulate a theoretical framework and apply it to your research paper.
    • To incorporate primary and secondary sources effectively into your research paper.
    • To demonstrate your expanded research skills by developing an annotated bibliography.
  • AI Policy: You may use AI in any way you see fit, provided it doesn't interfere with your achievement of these learning objectives. If you use AI, please write a brief (1-paragraph) discussion of how you used it.

Timeline

  • Week 11: Defining your topic and initial research.
  • Week 12: Refining Research and starting your Annotated Bibliography
  • Week 13: Your Theoretical Framework and expanding your Annotated Bibliography
  • Week 14: Incorporating Sources
  • Week 15: Preparing your Capstone Day Presentation
  • Week 16: Capstone Day Rehearsals

Deadlines

  • Annotated Bibliography due: Monday, April 6
  • Capstone Day Presentation: Thursday, April 30
  • Capstone Paper due: Wednesday, May 6 by 12:00 pm
