Week 7: Career Paths in Nonprofits

September 29, 2025

Monday, September 29

In class

  • Discussion:
    • Indeed.com nonprofit jobs
    • Globaljobs.com
    • Idealist.org
    • The 15 Biggest NGOs in the World
    • Save the Children Careers
    • OXFAM Careers

Assignment

  • Use the resources discussed in class:
    • Indeed.com nonprofit jobs
    • Globaljobs.com
    • Idealist.org
    • The 15 Biggest NGOs in the World
    • Save the Children Careers
    • OXFAM Careers
  • Identify 2-3 jobs/career paths you might be interested in applying to and write a brief forum post about them, ranking them in order of interest.
  • Post in our forum before next class.

Wednesday, October 1

  • Discussing jobs & career paths

Assignment

  • Write a post in which you discuss which nonprofit job you'd be interested in applying to, what it requires of you to successfully apply for it, and what career path you envision with it.
  • Post in our forum by noon on Friday.

Friday, October 3

Asynchronous Class Activity:
  • Read all of your classmates' posts and respond substantively (1-2 paragraphs) to at least 2 of them.
  • Your responses to classmates are due before class on Monday.
Assignment:
  • Explore Bookjobs.com
