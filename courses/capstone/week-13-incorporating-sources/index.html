Week 13: Incorporating Sources

November 10, 2025

Monday, November 10

In Class

  • Discussion of capstone outlines and drafts.
  • Incorporating secondary sources into your research paper.
  • Organizing your Capstone paper.
  • Capstone Paper Assignment

Assignment

  • Write a section of your essay in which you establish your theoretical framework and discuss how you will use it in the essay. Incorporate secondary sources as discussed in class today.
  • Post in our forum before class on Wednesday.

Wednesday, November 12

In Class

  • Discussion of your paper's theoretical framework.
  • Incorporating figures and images.

Assignment

  • Develop your draft further and share document on forum along with a paragraph describing your progress. Discuss any questions you might have, or challenges you have encountered along the way. How can we help you?
  • Post in our forum before noon on Friday.

Friday, November 14

Asynchronous Class Activity

  • Read your partner's summary paragraph, and draft. Offer helpful feedback.
  • Partners: Gia reads James, who reads Jessie, who reads Lydia, who reads Lillian, who reads Gia.

Assignment

  • Complete paper by Monday (or as close as you can).
  • Post link to Google Doc draft before class on Monday in forum, discussing how far along you are.
