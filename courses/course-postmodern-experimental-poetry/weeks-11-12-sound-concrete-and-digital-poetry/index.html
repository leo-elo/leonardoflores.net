Weeks 11 & 12: Sound, Concrete, and Digital Poetry

March 31, 2025

Monday, March 31

  • In class:
    • The Complete Works by Justin Stephenson. (poem) (trailer)
    • Brief Lecture on Sound Poetry
      • Hugo Ball "Seepferdchen und Flugfische" (2014) (gedichtvortrag) (Christian Bök)
      • Jim Andrews "Poetry Craft" (1989)
      • UbuWeb Sound
      • John Cage 4'33''
      • Kristin Oppenheim's sound poetry
      • Christian Bök's sound poetry
  • Assignment:
    • Read "Sound Poetry" in Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Read Paul de Vree’s Manifesto and sound poetry in Ubu Web.
    • Listen to the Happy New Ear album
    • Write a brief analysis of one sound poem. Provide a link to it in your response. Post before class on Wednesday.

Wednesday, April 1

  • In class:
    • Introduction to Concrete Poetry
      • Stéphane Mallarmé
      • Guillaume Apollinaire
      • French Letterists 1940s-1970s
      • Eugen Gomringer
      • Jonathan Williams
  • Assignment:
    • Read "Concrete Poetry" in Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Read “Pilot Plan for Concrete Poetry“
    • Explore works by Haroldo de Campos, Augusto de Campos, Decio Pignatari, and Mary Ellen Solt in UBUWEB: Historical.

Friday, April 3

  • Asynchronous class:
    • Write a detailed analysis of one concrete poem by one of the assigned poets and one example of a visually interesting use of language you have encountered, whether it’s on billboards, buildings, products, paintings, signs, graffiti, or any other form in the physical world. Include a link to the poem and a photograph, image, or screen capture. Post this analysis in ASULearn by 1:00 pm on Friday.
    • Read and respond substantively to at least 2 your classmates posts, and read their poems before class on Monday.
  • Assignment
    • Watch "First Screening" by bpNichol

Monday, April 7

  • In class:
    • Discussion of Concrete and Kinetic Poetry
      • First Screening by bpNichol
      • Typoems and Anipoems by Ana María Uribe (1997-99)
      • Strings by Dan Waber (1999)
      • #kinetictypography in Instagram
  • Assignment
    • Read "Electronic Poetry" in The Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Explore Lillian-Yvonne Bertram's
      • Website
      • Poetry Foundation page

Wednesday, April 9

  • Lecture and discussion of Lillian-Yvonne Bertram's work
    • ELO 2022 Readings
    • “for the pool players at the Golden Shovel” (Reference: We Real Cool)
    • “Weights and Measures”
  • Assignment:
    • Read:
      • Contexts:
        • "Incident" by Countee Cullen
        • Ben Johnson's Travesty Generator
        • Generative Pre-Trained Transformer
      • From Travesty Generator:
        • Statement on "Incident"
        • "Incident"
        • "Three Last Words"
      • poems from Negative Money

Friday, April 11

  • Asynchronous class:
    • Write a brief (250 words), but detailed post about Lillian-Yvonne Bertram's poetry and poetics, citing at least two examples of their poetry. Post by Saturday morning.
    • Read and respond substantively to at least 2 your classmates posts, and read their poems before class on Monday.
