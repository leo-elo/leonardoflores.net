Week 3: San Francisco, Beat, and Deep Image Poets

January 27, 2025

Monday, January 27

  • In class:
    • Gary Snyder’s poetry, "Riprap," "Endless Streams and Mountains"
    • Allen Ginsberg’s poetry, "A Supermarket in California"
  • Homework:
    • Readings:
      • "Howl" by Allen Ginsberg
      • "The Cut Up Method" by William Burroughs
      • "Cut-Up Poems from Minutes to Go" by William Burroughs
      • Read Jack Kerouac's bio and at least 2 poems.
    • Writing:
      • Write a 1-page analysis of "Howl" in which you discuss the poetics of the Beat movement and how it applies to the poem.

Wednesday, January 29

  • In class:
    • "Howl" by Allen Ginsberg (YouTube)
    • "The Cut Up Method" by William Burroughs
    • "Cut-Up Poems from Minutes to Go" by William Burroughs
    • Jack Kerouac's poetry.
    • Women of the Beat Generation
  • Homework:
    • Read "Deep Image" and "Surrealism" in The Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Read chapter on Robert Bly and his poetry in our textbook or in the Poetry Foundation website.

Friday, January 31

  • Online Asynchronous Class- class doesn't meet
    • Write a 250-word analysis in which you discuss how the poetics of the Deep Image school and characteristics of Surrealism manifest in a poem by Robert Bly. Post in our ASULearn discussion forum before 1:00 pm on Friday. Respond to 1 classmate in our forum before class on Monday.
  • Homework:
    • Readings:
      • "Confessional Poetry" in Princeton Encyclopedia
      • Robert Lowell's poems
      • Anne Sexton's poems
      • Watch Anne Sexton USA Poetry documentary.
    • Writing:
      • Analyze a poem by Robert Lowell or Anne Sexton and discuss it in the context of the poetics of Confessional Poetry.
      • Post before class on Monday.
