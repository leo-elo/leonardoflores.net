Weeks 9 & 10: Recorded and Performance Poetry

March 20, 2023

Monday, March 20

  • In class:
    • Introduction to audio recorded poetry
      • T.S. Eliot, "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock" (1917)
      • Robert Frost, "Fire and Ice" (1920)
      • Rita Dove, "Parsley" (1983)
    • The Last Poets:
      • "The Last Poets" (1970) (Album)(Lyrics)
      • "Party and Bullshit" (1993) - Biggie Smalls (lyrics)
      • "The Corner" (2005) - Common
  • Assignment:
    • Watch "Ursula Rucker: Poet" documentary
    • "Return to Innocence Lost" (1999)
    • Write a response to the documentary and "Return to Innocence Lost"

Wednesday, March 21

  • In class:
    • Ursula Rucker
      • "Supernatural" by Ursula Rucker (1994)
      • Ursula Rucker channel on YouTube
      • "What a Woman Must Do" - Def Poetry Jam
    • Amanda Gorman, "Using Your Voice Is a Political Choice" (2018)
    • Shane Koyczan "To this Day" (2013)
  • Assigment:
    • Read "Poetry Slam" and "Performance" in Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Watch at least 3 performance poems in YouTube and paying special attention on the aural and visual information conveyed through, voice, external dress, facial expressions, gestures, body language, etc.

Monday, March 27

  • Asynchronous class (we don't meet today):
    • Write a detailed analysis of one of the performance poems in YouTube, focusing on how the multimodality is meaningful in the poem. Include a link to the video. Incorporate screenshots and links to specific moments in the video (in YouTube, you can share a link to a specific second in the video), and analyze at least one moment in this. Post this analysis in ASULearn  before class today.
    • Read and respond substantively to at least 2 your classmates posts, and watch the performances, before class on Wednesday.
  • Assignment:
    • Watch "What is Multimodality?" video with Gunther Kress
    • Do a multimodal analysis of a music video you like, focusing on how all its different elements come together to communicate more than the sum of its parts (images, video, lyrics, performance, sound, etc.). Include a link to the music video. Incorporate screenshots and links to specific moments in the video (in YouTube, you can share a link to a specific second in the video), and analyze at least one moment in this.
    • Post before class on Wednesday.

Wednesday, March 29

  • In class:
    • Sharing and discussion of multimodal videos
    • Introduction to writing on video and videopoetry
      • The Art of the Title
      • “North by Northwest” (1959)
      • “Pinky and the Brain Title Sequence“ (1995)
      • "Fuck You" by Cee Lo Green (2010)
      • "Right Action" by Franz Ferdinand (2013)
      • "The Dreamlife of Letters" (2000) by Brian Kim Stefans
    •  
  • Assignment:
    • Watch “Introduction to the Concept and Theory of Videopoetry” by Tom Konyves
    • Watch “Sign Language” (1985) by Tom Konyves
    • Watch “When” (2009) by Ottar Ormstad
    • Write a brief response to the videopoems by Konyves and Ormstad. Post before class on Monday.
