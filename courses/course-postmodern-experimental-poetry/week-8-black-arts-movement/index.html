Week 8: Black Arts Movement

March 3, 2025

We want a black poem. And a Black World. Let the world be a Black Poem And Let All Black People Speak This Poem Silently or LOUD "Black Art" by Amiri Baraka

Monday, March 3

  • In class:
    • Introduction to the Black Arts Movement
    • Amiri Baraka (Poets.org) (Penn Sound)
      • "An Agony. As Now"
      • "Dope" (YouTube)
  • Assignment:
    • Read intro and poetry by:
      • Nikki Giovanni
      • Gwendolyn Brooks

Wednesday, March 5

  • In class:
    • Nikki Giovanni (Poetryfoundation.org)
      • "Ego Tripping" (YouTube)
      • "The Great Pax Whitie" (YouTube)
    • Gwendolyn Brooks
  • Assignment
    • Sonia Sanchez
    • Ishmael Reed

Friday, March 7

  • Midterm Self Assessment due (via ASULearn).
  • Short Essay: Poetics in Practice due (submit via ASULearn).
  • Asynchronous Online Class:
    • Write a 250-word post on Sonia Sanchez’ and Ishmael Reed’s approaches to poetry in the context of the Black Arts Movement. Reference and link to a poem for each.
    • Post in our forum before 1pm on Friday.
    • Respond to a classmate’s post by Monday.
  • Assignment
    • Listen to "The Last Poets" (1970) album (YouTube)(Spotify)(Lyrics)
    • Write a post in which you discuss what sound-- voice and music-- contribute to their poetry. How would their poetry be different if it was only published on the page? Post before class on Monday, March 17.
