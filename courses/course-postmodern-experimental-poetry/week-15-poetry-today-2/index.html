Week 15: Poetry Today

April 28, 2025

Monday, April 28

  • In class:
    • The Agrippa Files and “Agrippa” by William Gibson (1992)
  • Assignment:
    • Read at least 2 poems from Jason Edward Lewis' PoEMM cycle:
      • Visit poemm.net and then:
        • If you have an iPhone or iPad, download two of the apps and read them that way.
        • If you don't have an iOS device, then explore the works and watch the documentation videos.
      • Write a brief post in which you discuss two of the works, thinking about how the use of touchscreen interactivity and overall design contribute to the experience of his poems.
    • Work on your Final Project, which is due on Wednesday, April 30.

Wednesday, April 30

  • Final Project due.
  • In class:
    • Discussion of Jason Edward Lewis' PoEMM cycle
    • Discussion of "The Water Cave"
    • Digital poetry publication venues
      • The New River
      • Taper
      • Filter
      • The HTML Review
      • Ensemble Park
      • Using Electricity book series
      • The Electronic Literature Collection
      • The New Media Writing Prize
    • Final exam review
      • Read "The Art of Immemorability" by Charles Bernstein
      • Final exam will be online (take home) on Wednesday, May 7 from 1:00-3:00 pm and will be made available through ASULearn.
      • Here's a link to the exam I gave last time.
  • Assignment:
    • Complete your assignments and engagement self assessments
