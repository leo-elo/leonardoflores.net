February 17, 2025

  • In class:

    • Li Young Lee
    • Sharon Olds

    • Frank O’Hara bio and poems (textbook preferred).
    • John Ashbery bio and poems (textbook preferred).

Wednesday, February 19

  • In class:
    • Frank O’Hara - Why I Am Not a Painter, Poem
    • John Ashbery - Farm Implements and Rutabagas in a Landscape
    • NYC Trip and Alternate Assignments
  • Assignment:
    • Attend or watch livestream for Wordhack on Thursday, Feb. 20 7-10pm.
    • If you can't, then explore the websites for the featured artists (links to their sites above).

Friday, February 21

  • Asynchronous Online Class:
    • If you're in NYC, we will have a discussion about it at the Loft. No post necessary.
    • If not please post a 250-word discussion of how you see digital poetry by Constant Dullaart, Blake Andrews and Leonardo Flores expand the traditions of Modernism and Postmodernism.
    • Post in our forum before 1pm on Friday.
    • Respond to a classmate's post by Monday.
  • Assignment:
    • "Language Poetry" in Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Read Charles Bernstein's bio and poems (textbook preferred).
