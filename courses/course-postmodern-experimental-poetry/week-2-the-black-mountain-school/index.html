Week 2: The Black Mountain School

January 21, 2025

Monday, January 20

  • State holiday: MLK Day - class doesn't meet.

Wednesday, January 22

  • In class:
    • Lecture and discussion on Black Mountain School
      • "Buffalo Bill 's" by E.E. Cummings.
      • "[from] Asphodel, That Greeny Flower" by William Carlos Williams
      • "Projective Verse" by Charles Olson.
      • Charles Olson and his poems here.
      • Charles Olson in PennSound
  • Assignment:
    • Readings:
      • Robert Duncan's bio and poetry (in textbook or in the link provided). Duncan @ PennSound
      • Robert Creeley's bio and poetry (in textbook or in the link provided). Creeley @ PennSound
      • Denise Levertov's bio and poetry (in textbook or in the link provided). Levertov video
    • Writing:
      • See instructions for asynchronous class on Friday

Friday, January 24

  • Asynchronous class - no meeting.
    • Write a 250-word analysis in which you discuss how the poetics of the Black Mountain School manifest in a poem by two out of three assigned poets: Robert Creeley, Robert Duncan, or Denise Levertov. Post in our ASULearn discussion forum before 1:00 pm on Friday. Respond to 2 classmates in our forum before class on Monday.
  • Assignment:
    • "San Francisco Renaissance" and "Beat Poets" in The Princeton Encyclopedia of Poetry and Poetics.
    • Gary Snyder's bio and two of his poems (in textbook or in the link provided)
    • Allen Ginsberg's bio and two of his poems (in textbook or in the link provided)
