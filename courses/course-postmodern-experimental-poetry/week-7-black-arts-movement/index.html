Week 7: Black Arts Movement

March 2, 2021

We want a black poem. And a
Black World.
Let the world be a Black Poem
And Let All Black People Speak This Poem
Silently
or LOUD

"Black Art" by Amiri Baraka

Tuesday, March 2

  • In class:
    • Introduction to the Black Arts Movement
    • Amiri Baraka (Poets.org) (Penn Sound)
    • Nikki Giovanni (Poetryfoundation.org) ("Ego Tripping") (Tedx Talk)
  • Assignment:
    • Read Black Arts Movement in Princeton Encyclopedia
    • Read intro and poetry by Ishmael Reed and Gwendolyn Brooks
    • Watch Nikki Giovanni's Tedx Talk (link), respond to it in general here, and annotate "Ego Tripping" (link) by selecting excerpts and commenting on them. I encourage you to reply to your classmates' comments, to the point of having a conversation around the poem.

Thursday, March 4

  • In class:
    • Ishmael Reed
    • Gwendolyn Brooks
  • Assignment:
    • Read "New York School" in Princeton Encyclopedia.
    • Read "Personism: A Manifesto" by Frank O'Hara
    • Read Frank O'Hara bio and poems.
    • Read John Ashbery bio and poems.
