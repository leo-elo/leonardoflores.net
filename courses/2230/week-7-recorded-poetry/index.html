Week 7: Recorded Poetry

September 29, 2020

Tuesday, September 29

  • In class (via Zoom)
    • Lecture and discussion of recorded poetry:
      • "We Real Cool" by Gwendolyn Brooks
      • "When the Revolution Comes" (YouTube) by The Last Poets
      • "l.o.v.e" by Ursula Rucker
      • "Seahorses and Flying Fish" by Hugo Ball
      • Happy New Ear (1995) album
  • Homework
    • Explore the following resources:
      • PennSound and poetry recordings
      • Library of Congress Archive of Recorded Poetry and Literature
      • Write a 1-paragraph response in which you discuss what kinds of information that you cannot capture with writing you notice in the recorded poetry you've listened to. List the poet(s) you've listened to. This is due before 3:30 pm on Thursday.

Thursday, October 1

  • In class (Asynchronous class - we don't meet)
    • Explore the work of one poet in UbuWeb Sound
    • Write a 1-page analysis of one work of sound poetry, by that poet providing a link to the work. Include a brief paragraph on your impression of the poet's overall work and approach to sound poetry, based on what you have heard. The title of your document should be the title and author of the poem. This is due by midnight on Friday, October 2.
    • Read 2 of your classmates' posts and comment substantively no later than midnight on Monday, October 5.

Class on Tuesday, October 6 will also be asynchronous.
