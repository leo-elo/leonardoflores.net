Week 10: Videopoetry

Tuesday, October 20

In class:

  • Young-Hae Chang Heavy Industries
  • “First Screening” by bpNichol
  • “Typoemas and Anipoemas” by Ana María Uribe
  • “When” by Ottar Ormstad
  • “The Complete Works” by bpNichol

Assignment (for next class):

  • Watch “The Complete Works” (2015) by Justin Stephenson
  • Analyze 2 of the sections of “The Complete Works” focusing on how Stephenson presents language poetically in each section.

Thursday, October 22

In class:

  • “The Complete Works” by Justin Stephenson
  • “Velocity” (2000) by Tina Escaja
  • “Mastering the Art of French Cooking and Systems Theory” (2015) by Tan Lin
  • “Thoughts Go” (2000) by David Knoebel

Assignment (for next class):

  • Read Rettberg, Electronic Literature, Chapter 5: Kinetic and Interactive Poetry
  • Watch/Read:
    • “Acts in Translation” by Dalena Tran
    • “Bring Back” by Rosamond King
    • “When” by Ottar Ormstad
  • Write a brief forum post discussing what you found to be most effective about these videopoems.
