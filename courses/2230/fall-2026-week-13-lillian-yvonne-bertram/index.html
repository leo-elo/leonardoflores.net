Week 13: Lillian Yvonne Bertram

November 10, 2026

Tuesday, November 10

  • In class:
    • Lecture and discussion of Lillian-Yvonne Bertram’s work
      • Website
      • Poetry Foundation page
      • “for the pool players at the Golden Shovel” (Reference: We Real Cool)
      • “Weights and Measures”
  • Assignment:
    • Read:
      • ELO 2022 Readings
    • Write a post in which you discuss two of Lillian-Yvonne Bertram’s digital poems, focusing on how the poem’s design enhances the content.

Thursday, November 12

In class:

  • Lecture and discussion of Lillian-Yvonne Bertram’s work
    • ELO 2022 Readings

Assignment:

  • Read:
    • Contexts:
      • “Incident” by Countee Cullen
      • Ben Johnson’s Travesty Generator
      • Generative Pre-Trained Transformer
    • From Travesty Generator:
      • Statement on “Incident”
      • “Incident”
      • “Three Last Words”
    • poems from Negative Money

Asynchronous activities:

  • Read: excerpts from Travesty Generator and Negative Money (available here)
  • Write a post in which you discuss at least two of Lillian-Yvonne Bertram’s Travesty Generator and Negative Money poems in support of a larger argument about how their poetic practice engages computations in interesting ways.
  • Post by Saturday, respond to a classmate by Tuesday.
