Week 12: Jason Edward Lewis

November 3, 2026

Tuesday, November 3

  • In class:
    • Research Paper
    • Lecture and discussion of Jason Edward Lewis’ work
      • Website
      • PoEMM Website (has videos with interactions)
      • PoEMM: The Album
  • Assignment:
    • Read:
      • PoEMM: The Album
    • Write a post in which you discuss two of Jason Edward Lewis’ poems, focusing on how the poem’s form matches the content.

Thursday, November 5

In class:

  • Lecture and discussion of Jason Edward Lewis’ work
    • Website
    • PoEMM: The Album

Assignment:

  • Read:
    • PoEMM: The Album
  • Write a post in which you discuss another one of Jason Edward Lewis’ poems, focusing on how the poem’s form matches the content.
  • Watch “The Future Imaginary”

Asynchronous activities:

  • Write a post about how Jason Edward Lewis’ work, both in the PoEMM cycle and overall, engages with issues of indigenous identity and how that matches his overall poetic practice. Reference at least one essay from PoEMM: The Album.
  • Post by Saturday, reply to a classmate’s post by Tuesday.
