Weeks 2-3: Computational Writing

Tuesday

In class:

  • Memory Slam
  • Strategies for reading computational writing

Assignment:

  • Read
    • The Marble Canyons in Beautiful Taroko National Park, Taiwan
    • Nick Montfort, “Taroko Gorge”
  • Write a brief discussion of how Montfort’s poem seeks to capture the experience of the Taroko gorge and how he ends the poem. Post before class on Thursday.

Thursday

In class:

  • The Marble Canyons in Beautiful Taroko National Park, Taiwan
  • Nick Montfort, “Taroko Gorge”
  • Nick Montfort, “Taroko Gorge” (remix link version)

Assignment:

  • Watch “Distant Writing” (recorded lecture)
  • Explore NaNoGenMo, using my NaNoGenMo Guide to read/explore 8-10 novels.
  • Write a post in which you apply the concept of distant writing as discussed in my lecture to 3 NaNoGenMo works. Post before our next class.

Tuesday

In class:

  • NaNoGenMo & NaNoGenMo Guide

Assignment:

  • Read:
    • ”Notes on the Voyage of Owl and Girl” by J.R. Carpenter
    • ”Who Were You Before Your Parents Were Born” by Loss Pequeño Glazier
    • ”Sea and Spar Between” by Stephanie Strickland and Nick Montfort
  • Write a detailed analysis of the poetic and computational strategies used in one of the assigned poems. Post before class.

Thursday

In class:

  • ”Notes on the Voyage of Owl and Girl” by J.R. Carpenter
  • ”Who Were You Before Your Parents Were Born” by Loss Pequeño Glazier
  • ”Sea and Spar Between” by Stephanie Strickland and Nick Montfort

Assignment:

  • Read
    • Explore Finding ELIZA
    • ”The lives and afterlives of community-created bots on Twitter: A minor history”
    • Explore the bot collection in the Electronic Literature Collection, Vol. 3
    • Write a reaction/response to two of the bots you read in the collection. This is due before class.
