Week 7: Keith Wilson’s Poetry and Poetics

September 30, 2026

Tuesday, September 30

  • In class:
    • Discuss Creative Project #2
    • Keith Wilson’s poetry:
      • Black Matters
      • I Find Myself Defending Pigeons
      • line dance for an american textbook
      • Processing Emmett Till (Uncanny Emmett Till #2)
      • reportage on a theory
      • Selected Work
  • Assignment:
    • Read:
      • “Experimental Digital Poetics” by Keith S. Wilson
    • Write a reaction/response to the assigned works in which you discuss how interactivity is meaningful in the work.

Thursday, October 2

In class:

  • “Experimental Digital Poetics” by Keith S. Wilson

Assignment:

  • Do one of the following:
    • Watch this interview and read at least 3 of his selected works

Asynchronous activities:

  • In our forum, write a 250 word (approximately) post in which you discuss Keith S. Wilson’s poetic style and approaches to writing poetry, including his use of poetic form.
  • This is due by Saturday morning.
  • Respond to a classmate’s post by Tuesday.

Assignment:

  • Read “High Muck a Muck” by Fred Wah, Nicola Harwood, Jin Zhang, Bessie Wapp, Thomas Loh, Tomoyo Ihaya, Hiromoto Ida, Phillip Djwa, and Patrice Leung
  • Write a response to this work, focusing on how the work is designed and to what effect.

Also on Thursday, October 2:

  • Discuss:
    • Keith Wilson’s poetry
    • “High Muck a Muck”
    • Creative Project #2
    • Midterm Self Assessment
  • Demos:
    • Twine
    • Bitsy
    • Inklewriter

Assignment:

  • Read:
    • “Patterns of Hypertext” by Mark Bernstein
    • Work on Creative Project #2
  • Write your Midterm Self Assessment
