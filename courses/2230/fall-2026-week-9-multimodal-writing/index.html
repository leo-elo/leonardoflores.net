Week 9: Multimodal Writing

Tuesday, October 13

In class:

  • Discussion of “Welcome to Pine Point” and multimodality.
  • Gunther Kress theory of multimodality (“Gains and losses: New forms of texts, knowledge, and learning” 2005)
  • Key concepts in multimodality:
    • Mode – A socially shaped and culturally recognized resource for making meaning (e.g., image, writing, gesture, music, layout).
    • Modal Affordance – What it is possible to express easily with a particular mode, shaped by its physical material and cultural history.
    • Sign-Maker – The individual who actively forms signs based on their specific social interest in a given moment, rather than passively using pre-made codes.
    • Transduction – The process of moving meaning from one mode to a different mode (e.g., speech to visual drawing).
  • Title sequences in Film and Television:
    • Title Sequence for North by Northwest (1959) by Arthur Preminger
    • Pinky and the Brain Title Sequence (1995) by Bryan Evans, Tom Ruegger, et. al.
    • Cowboy Bebop (1998) by Cain Kuga

Assignment (for next class):

  • Watch/Read:
    • “Introduction to the Concept and Theory of Videopoetry” by Tom Konyves
    • “Sign Language” (1985) by Tom Konyves
  • Write a brief analysis of “Sign Language” in the context of Konyves’ theory of videopoetry. Post before our next class.
