Week 1: What is Electronic Literature? And who gets to write it?

August 21, 2023

Monday, August 21

In class:

  • Welcome and introductions
  • Syllabus and course overview
  • What is electronic/digital literature?
    • “Loveletters” by Christopher Strachey (1952)
    • “Correcaminos” by Eduardo Darino (1965)

Assignment:

  • Read
    • Rettberg. Electronic Literature, Chapter 1: Genres of Electronic Literature
    • Wikipedia contributors. "ELIZA" and "PARRY"
    • Garber. "When PARRY Met ELIZA: A Ridiculous Chatbot Conversation From 1972"
  • Interact with ELIZA
  • In our forum, write a brief reaction/response to the assigned material, discussing what you think chatbots may contribute to literature.

Wednesday, August 23

In class:

  • Discussion of chatbots and their potential contributions to literature.
  • Discussion of electronic literature genres.
    • bp Nichol. "First Screening" (1982)
    • Judy Malloy. "Uncle Roger" (1986)

Assignment:

  • Materials
    • Watch Flores "Third Generation Electronic Literature" (2018)
    • Wikipedia contributors. "Colossal Cave Adventure"
    • Play Crowther and Woods, "Colossal Cave Adventure" (1976)
  • In our forum, write a brief reaction/response to the electronic literature genres and examples discussed, thinking about how they go beyond the capabilities of print. Reference some of the works assigned, so far.

Friday, August 25

Asynchronous class:

  • Watch Flores, “Digital Writing and Digital Divides in the US: Electronic Literature and Privilege”
  • Read
    • About Eduardo Kac
    • “Digital Poetry” by Eduardo Kac (1982-present)
    • About Ana María Uribe
    • “Typoems and Anipoems” by Ana María Uribe (1997-2001)
  • In our forum, write a 250 word (approximately) post in which you discuss the work of Brazilian poet Eduardo Kac and Argentinian poet Ana María Uribe in the context of my lecture on digital writing and the other work we've covered so far. This is due by Saturday morning.
  • Read and respond to one classmate's postings before class on Monday.

Assignment:

  • Read
    • Rettberg, Electronic Literature, Chapter 2: Combinatory Poetics
    • The Marble Canyons in Beautiful Taroko National Park, Taiwan
    • Nick Montfort, "Taroko Gorge"
  • In our forum, write a brief discussion of how Montfort's poem seeks to capture the experience of the Taroko gorge and how he ends the poem.
