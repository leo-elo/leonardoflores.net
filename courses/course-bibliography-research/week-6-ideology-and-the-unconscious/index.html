Week 6: Ideology and the Unconscious

September 23, 2021

In class

  • Discussion of Psychoanalytic Theories and Criticism:
    • Freud - model of the mind, mechanisms (repression, suppression, condensation, displacement), manifest and latent content - Adrienne Rich "Aunt Jennifer's Tigers"
    • Lacan - the mirror stage, the imaginary, the symbolic, and the real - Jorge Luis Borges, "On Exactitude in Science"
    • Kristeva - chora, semiotic, symbolic - EDM "Nyan Cat", Helen Chasin "The Word Plum"
  • Discussion of Marxist Theory
    • Marx and Engels - material dialectic, estrangement, commodity, ideology, hegemony - Langston Hughes "Bad Man", Trainspotting opening scene
    • Jameson - "Always historicize!", metacommentary, semantic horizons (explication, ideologeme, ideology of form) - Brazil opening scene
    • Žižek - "opening scene" "Starbucks scene"
  • Discussion of Annotated Bibliography and research methods

Assignments

  • Complete your Annotated Bibliography
  • Readings:
    • "Cultural Studies and New Historicism" (26-28)
    • Stuart Hall (1895-1910)
    • Roland Barthes (1457-1465)
    • Stephen Greenblatt (2250-2254)
    • Jean Baudrillard "from Simulacra and Simulation"
  • Watch The Matrix (1999)
  • Discuss The Matrix in the context of the assigned theoretical approaches. Due before class on Thursday, September 30.
