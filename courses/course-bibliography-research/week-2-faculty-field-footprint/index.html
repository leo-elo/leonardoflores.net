Week 2: Faculty, Field, Footprint

August 26, 2021

In class

  • Getting to know our faculty:
    • Visit: https://english.appstate.edu/faculty-staff
      • Table 1: Atkinson to Flores
      • Table 2: Groover to Wahpeconiah
      • Table 3: Wilson to Searfoss
      • Table 4: Vernon to Mannon
    • Share your findings with the class in a 1-2 minute presentation.
  • Conversations:
    • The field: history, major areas, specializations
    • Academia: tenure-stream vs non tenure stream, rank, job market expectations
  • Your Digital Footprint:
    • Exercise: Look yourself up. What can you discover about yourself online? What social networks you have accounts in?
    • Managing your online identity, creating or curating your digital footprint:
      • ORCID
      • Humanities Commons
      • HASTAC
      • Reclaim Hosting
      • Social Networks with Academic Presence: Twitter, Facebook
      • Google Scholar

Assignments

  • Readings:
    • Viktor Shklovsky "Art as Technique"
    • Cleanth Brooks and William K. Wimsatt, Jr. (1350-1402)
    • Roland Barthes "The Death of the Author" (1466-1469)
    • Louise Rosenblatt "Transactional Theory in the Teaching of Literature"
    • Wolfgang Iser (1670-1681)
  • Writing Assignment:
    • Write a 1-2 page summary of the key concepts of Russian Formalism (Shklovsky), New Criticism (Brooks, Wimsatt, Barthes), and Reader's Response Theory (Rosenblatt, Iser).
    • Post this by Tuesday, August 30 in Google Classroom.
    • Read and comment on two classmates' postings before class on Thursday.

Class meets in Belk Library room 024 next week.
