Week 11: Digital Humanities

October 28, 2021

In Class

  • Assigned exploration and readings:
    • Debates in the Digital Humanities (2011)
      • Read Chapter 1: "What Is Digital Humanities and What’s It Doing in English Departments?" by Matthew G. Kirschenbaum.
    • Debates in the Digital Humanities (2016)
    • Debates in the Digital Humanities (2019)
  • Guides:
    • WCU Digital Humanities
    • Wikipedia entry on DH
  • Text Processing & Analysis:
    • Markov chain generators
    • Text topology
    • Voyant
  • Methodologies:
    • Text Encoding - XML/TEI in 5 Minutes, TEI
    • Topic modeling - Topic Modeling: Introduction
    • Distant reading - Franco Moretti video
    • Digital Archives & Publication - Walden: A Fluid Text Edition, The Electronic Literature Collection
    • Visualization - Torn Apart
  • Projects:
    • DH Awards
    • Digital Pedagogy in the Humanities

Assignment

  • Readings:
    • "Print is Flat, Code is Deep: The Importance of Media-Specific Analysis" by N. Katherine Hayles
    • Watch lecture on "Third Generation Electronic Literature" and read "Third Generation Electronic Literature" by Leonardo Flores
    • Explore The Electronic Literature Collection, volumes 1-3.
  • Writing:
    • How do these works' media-specificity move away from the media-specific constraints of the print tradition? Respond to at least three works of electronic literature in the electronic literature collections. Due before class.
