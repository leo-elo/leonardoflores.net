Week 6

High Modernism

Reminder: Short Essay is due on Friday, Oct. 11.

Tuesday, September 24

In class

  • Lecture and discussion of High Modernism
  • Discussion of W.B. Yeats and his poetry (resource)

Assignment

  • Read "Tradition and the Individual Talent" and "From Hamlet" in our textbook (pg 941-949).
  • Read "Objective Correlative" in The Princeton Encyclopedia of Poetry and Poetics
  • Read chapter on T.S. Eliot all the way to Gerontion (don't read The Waste Land yet). Pay special attention to "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock" and "The Preludes."
  • Write a forum post in which you discuss how Eliot uses the objective correlative in "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock" or "The Preludes."

Thursday, September 26

In class

  • Lecture and discussion of T.S. Eliot's poetry
  • Introduction to "The Waste Land"

Assignment

  • Read "The Waste Land" parts 1-3 and all the footnotes in our textbook.
  • Write a forum post in which you offer a summary of the main ideas in the section assigned to you and the first half of the poem, overall. Post in our forum before class on Tuesday.
