Week 5

Make it New

Tuesday, September 17

In class

  • Discussion of Ezra Pound, Vorticism, Blast, and Modernism.
  • Read the rest of "A Retrospect" by Ezra Pound (pgs. 429-433 in our textbook or here)

Assignment

  • Read Gertrude Stein's "From A Transatlantic Interview" in our textbook (pg 987).
  • Read the Wikipedia entry on Cubism.
  • Read chapter on Gertrude Stein and her poems in our textbook. (Alternatively, read this resource, and the poems it links to.)
  • Write a forum post in which you discuss a poem by Stein, focusing on how she manifests the ideas of Modernism or Cubism in her poem. Post before class on Thursday.

Thursday, September 19

In class - class meets via Zoom link in ASULearn

  • Briefly state your poem for Essay #1.
  • Discussion of Gertrude Stein's poetry, Cubism, and Modernism.

Assignment

  • Read chapter on William Butler Yeats and his poems in our textbook.
  • Read "Symbolism" in The Princeton Encyclopedia of Poetry and Poetics
  • Write a forum post in which you discuss a poem by W.B. Yeats and his use of symbolism. Post in our forum before class on Tuesday.
