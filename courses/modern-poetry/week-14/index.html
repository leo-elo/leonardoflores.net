Week 14

Digital Modernism

Tuesday, November 19

In class

  • Discussion of Digital Modernism by Jessica Pressman (access through Library)
  • Discussion of Memory Slam 2.0 by Nick Montfort

Assignment

  • Read the Wikipedia entry on Young-Hae Chang Heavy Industries (YHCHI)
  • Read DAKOTA by Young-Hae Chang Heavy Industries
  • Read "Speed Reading: SUPER-POSITION AND SIMULTANEITY in Young-hae Chang Heavy Industries’s Dakota and Ezra Pound’s Cantos" Chapter 3 in Digital Modernism.
  • Explore yhchang.com
  • Write a a brief post on in which you discuss how YHCHI's poetry is Modernist.

Thursday, November 21

In Class

  • Discussion of YHCHI's poetry
    • "The Last Day of Betty Nknomo"
    • "Bust Down the Doors!"
  • "Sign Language" by Tom Konyves
  • "Right Action" by Franz Ferdinand and Jonah Odell
  • "Thoughts Go" by David Knoebel

Assignment

  • Read:
    • "Seattle Drift" by Jim Andrews
    • "Slipping Glimpse" by Stephanie Strickland and Cynthia Lawson Jaramillo
    • "Game, Game, Game, and Again Game" by Jason Nelson
    • "Emblem/as" by Tina Escaja
  • Write a post in which you discuss how these works innovate in their use of poetic form in a way that registers as Modernist. How does form shape the content? Post before class on Tuesday.
