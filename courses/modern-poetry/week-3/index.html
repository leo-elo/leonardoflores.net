Week 3

American Regionalism

Tuesday, September 3

In class

  • Discussion of American Regionalism
  • Edgar Lee Masters. (resource)

Assignment

  • Read chapter on E.A. Robinson and all his poems in our textbook. (Alternatively, read this resource, and all of the poems it links to.)
  • Read Stanza in The Princeton Encyclopedia of Poetry and Poetics.
  • Write a 250 word essay in which you offer an interpretation of one of E.A. Robinson's poems, and then analyze how he used poetic form and aspects of sound strategically in ways that support that interpretation. Consider stanza structure, rhyme, rhythm, metrical patterns, alliteration, consonance, assonance, repetition of words, phrases, lines, etc. in your analysis. Don't try to hit all points in the essay. Analyze everything, but then use what contributes to your analysis and argument.

Thursday, September 5

In class

  • Asynchronous class - Online activity. Class does not meet.
  • Post your 250-word essay before class (description above).
  • Read 2 classmates' essays and Robinson poems-- at least one should be on a different poem than you wrote about-- and respond substantively, elaborating on the poetic devices you notice in the poems. This is due by the end of the day Friday, September 6.

Assignment

  • Read Imagism in The Princeton Encyclopedia of Poetry and Poetics, at least as far as the section on Modern Poetry.
  • Read "A Retrospect" (up to, but not including Prolegomena) by Ezra Pound (pgs. 429-433 in our textbook or here).
  • Read chapter on H.D. and her poems in our textbook. (Alternatively, read this resource, and the poems it links to.)
  • Write a forum post in which you discuss an Imagist poem by H.D. (hint: not all her poems are Imagist) and how it exemplifies the ideas of Imagism. Post in our forum before class on Tuesday.
