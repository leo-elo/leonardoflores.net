Week 7: Writers, Readers, Players, Wreaders

February 26, 2019

Tuesday, February 26

  • In class
    • Discussion of assigned works:
      • Jason Nelson: "Game, Game, Game, and Again Game," "Nothing You Have Done Deserves Such Praise" or "A Nervous System."
      • Christine Wilks: "Fitting the Pattern," "Tailspin," or "Inkubus."
      • Michael Mateas and Andrew Stern: "Façade."
    • Procedural Rhetoric: GOP Arcade, Grita.
  • Assignment
    • Install, read/play one work from each bullet listed below:
      • One of Jason Edward Lewis and Bruno Nadeau's PoEMM apps (iOS), one of Jörg Piringer's App Poems (iOS), or #Carnivast by Dreaming Methods and Mez Breeze (Android).
      • Pry by Tender Claws (iOS) or "How to Rob a Bank" by Alan Bigelow (cross-platform).
      • Out There (cross-platform), Voyageur (iOS & Android), or Reigns (cross-platform).
    • Write a post in our Facebook Group in which you discuss the three works you read, and talk about what the interactivity and/or procedural rhetoric (when applicable) contribute to each work. Post your responses before class on Thursday, February 28.

Thursday, February 28

  • In class:
    • Discuss 3rd Generation interactive e-lit.
    • Midterm Exam Review
  • Assignment:
    • Share your idea for the Major Assignment #1 as a comment on this Facebook post before the end of Friday, March 1.
    • Study for the Midterm Exam (on Tuesday, March 5)
    • Work on your Major Assignment #1 (due Thursday, March 7)
