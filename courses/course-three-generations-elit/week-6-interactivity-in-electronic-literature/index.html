Week 6: Interactivity in Electronic Literature

February 19, 2019

Tuesday, February 19

  • In class
    • Discussion of hypertext, interactive fiction, Twine Games
      • Galatea Cheats and Walkthroughs
      • A Duck Has An Adventure
    • Interactivity in electronic literature
  • Assignment
    • Read Electronic Literature pgs. 104-109.
    • Download and play The Stanley Parable Demo (requires PC & Steam. If unable to, watch this playthrough.
    • Watch/Play Black Mirror: Bandersnatch (available on Netflix)
    • Write a post in our Facebook Group in which you respond to The Stanley Parable Demo and Bandersnatch. Post your responses before class on Thursday, February 21.

Thursday, February 21

  • In class
    • Discussion Bandersnatch and The Stanley Parable Demo
  • Assignment
    • Read Electronic Literature pgs. 109-117.
    • Read/Play at least one work from each bullet in this list:
      • Jason Nelson: "Game, Game, Game, and Again Game,""Nothing You Have Done Deserves Such Praise" or "A Nervous System."
      • Christine Wilks: "Fitting the Pattern,""Tailspin," or "Inkubus."
      • Michael Mateas and Andrew Stern: "Façade."
    • Write a post in our Facebook Group in which you respond to "Façade," one work by Jason Nelson, and one by Christine Wilks. Post your responses before class on Tuesday, February 26.
