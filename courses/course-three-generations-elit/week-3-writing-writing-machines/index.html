Week 3: Writing Writing Machines

January 29, 2019

Tuesday, January 29

  • In class:
    • Discussion of combinatorial poetry
    • Discussion:
      • "The Policeman's Beard is Half Constructed" and Markov Chain Generators.
      • "Taroko Gorge"
      • "Taroko Gorge and its Remixes"
      • Taper
  • Assignment:
    • Readings:
      • Electronic Literature, pgs. 51-53
      • Explore one or several editions of "NaNoGenMo" (click on the "Issues" on the top menu to see the novels) and sample some of the completed novels.
      • Explore the Bot Collection in the ELC3.
      • Use the "What Would I Say?" app on your Facebook account.
    • Write a 1-paragraph response to one of the NaNoGenMo novels that most drew your attention. Post your response as a comment on this post in Facebook Group, along with a link to the work you responded to. Your response is due before class on Thursday.

Thursday, January 31

  • In class:
    • Discussion of 3rd generation combinatorial literature.
  • Assignment:
    • Readings:
      • Electronic Literature, pgs. 54-68.
      • "The Garden of Forking Paths" by Jorge Luis Borges
      • Read Wikipedia entry on Choose Your Own Adventure books.
      • Read Underground Kingdom by Edward Packard (I recommend downloading the PDF).
    • Write a 1-paragraph response to The Underground Kingdom or "The Garden of Forking Paths." Post it in our Facebook Group before class on Tuesday.
