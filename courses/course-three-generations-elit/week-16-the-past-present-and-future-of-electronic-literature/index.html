Week 16: The Past, Present, and Future of Electronic Literature

May 1, 2019

Tuesday, April 30

  • In class:
    • Presentation and discussion of "Third Generation Electronic Literature."
  • Assignment:
    • Find a work of 3rd generation e-lit not presented in class or published in any electronic literature collection or other resource, add a link to it as a comment on this post, and explain its interest from an e-literary perspective. This is due before class on Thursday, May 2.

Thursday, May 2

  • In class:
    • Discussion of e-lit and the e-literary in the wild.
    • Final exam review.
  • Assignment:
    • Study for the final exam!
    • Bonus Opportunities (due before the Final Exam):
      • Read and comment on Waliya Yohanna Joseph's e-poetry using this link.
      • Read and comment on "The Forever Club" by Alan Bigelow using this link.
