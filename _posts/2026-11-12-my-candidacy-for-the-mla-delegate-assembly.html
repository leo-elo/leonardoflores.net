I am interested in serving the MLA community as a Professional Issues delegate representing Distance and Online Education.

Here’s why:

  • I have been teaching, researching, and creating digital literature for over 25 years, and have valuable experience and perspective on AI and its impact on teaching, research, learning, assessment, and policy, including online education.
  • I’ve been developing artisanal course websites since 2003, using platforms like HTML, Blogger, Tumblr, Scalar, Wordpress, Jeckyll, and Claude Code.
    • You can see a selection of my course websites at https://leonardoflores.net/courses/
    • All my course websites are open access and have Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licenses.
  • I’m experienced with multiple LMS systems, such as WebCT, Blackboard, Moodle, and Canvas.
  • I have taught courses in many modalities relevant to this area, such as:
    • online synchronous and asynchronous (sometimes a combination),
    • a variety of hybrid courses modalities, and
    • hyflex/blended courses.
  • I am a creator of online educational resources, such as:
    • I ♥️ E-Poetry
    • The Electronic Literature Collection, Volume 3
    • Antología Lit(e)Lat, Volumen 1
  • I have served the MLA community for the past 4 years offering guidance on AI in the following capacities:
    • MLA-CCCC Joint Task Force on Writing and AI (2023–25),
    • MLA Task Force on AI in Research and Teaching (2025–present),
    • I co-chaired the AI in Research Working Group, which published “AI and the Humanities: A Framework for Language and Literary Scholarship.”

For more details, please take a moment to look at my CV and website: https://leonardoflores.net.

Vote by December 10!
