On June 30, 2026 I hosted and participated in an MLA Webinar titled "AI and the Humanities: A Framework for Language and Literary Scholarship" in which panelists John Murray, Rita Raley, Lisa Marie Rhody, and Md Mahmudul Haque discussed a recently published framework produced by the AI in Research Working Group.

Whether you have read the framework or not, this webinar is designed to show ways in which the framework can help us think through use cases and scenarios concerning the use of AI in research and scholarship. You will also learn about our process in creating this document.
