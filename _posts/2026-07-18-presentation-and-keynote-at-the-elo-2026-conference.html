Talk: Slipping into the Future

My first talk was part of a panel titled Reorienting Electronic Literature, collaborating with Mehulkumar Desai and Dr. Shanmugapriya T.

When I proposed this talk, I didn’t know that I would be invited to offer a keynote at this conference, and because I really wanted to be able to address AI in more detail in the keynote, I shaped this presentation as a critical/creative preamble to it.
