The Electronic Literature Collection, Volume 3 is an anthology of 114 works of electronic literature published by the Electronic Literature Organization in February 2016.

As one of the editors of this collection, I worked with co-editors Stephanie Boluk, Jacob Garbe, and Anastasia Salter to select, curate, and present works that represent the diversity and vitality of electronic literature from around the world.

The collection includes works in multiple languages and from many countries, featuring poetry generators, interactive fiction, kinetic poetry, games, and other forms of born-digital literary art.
